About This Project

This project is a web-based collection of engineering calculators designed to provide quick and accurate results for common structural engineering tasks, based on US and Brazilian codes.

The goal is to create a free, open-source, and easy-to-use tool for students and professionals. The calculators are intended for preliminary design and educational purposes.

Disclaimer

These tools are for preliminary design and educational purposes only. Always verify results with a licensed professional engineer and the latest code standards before use in any real-world application.
