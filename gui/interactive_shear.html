Shear Mechanics & Parameters Guide

Fundamental physical phenomena governing concrete shear capacity without stirrups

Size Effect (Efeito de Escala - α)

(d_dg / d)^α

Larger members (d > 600mm) fail at significantly lower average shear stresses due to wider critical crack openings, which reduce aggregate interlock. The factor (d_dg / d)^α counteracts this by accounting for aggregate roughness.

Length & Shear Span Analysis (a/d - δ)

(a / d)^δ

The a/d ratio represents the length analysis of the shear span relative to effective depth. When a/d < 2.5 (short span), load is transferred directly to supports via an inclined compressive strut (arch action / biela direta), resulting in much higher capacity. For a/d ≥ 2.5 (long span), standard beam flexure-shear action governs.

Longitudinal Reinforcement (Taxa de Armadura ρw - β)

(100·ρ_w)^β

Longitudinal reinforcement provides dowel action across the diagonal crack and restrains crack opening, preserving friction and aggregate interlock.

Concrete Strength (Resistência fck - γ)

fck^γ

Shear failure is brittle and governed by tensile strength fct, which scales with fck^(1/3) or fck^(1/2), as well as the strength of the intact compressive zone.

Geometry & Scale (bw^ε · d^ζ)

bw^ε · d^ζ

O produto geométrico bw^ε · d^ζ converte a tensão nominal resistente em força cortante nominal resistente. Tipicamente ε ≈ 1.0 e ζ ≈ 1.0 (área cisalhada bw·d).

Statistical Evaluation: Safety Margin (R) & Dispersion (CoV)

R = V_test / V_calc

R = V_test / V_calc represents model safety (R ≥ 1.0 is safe). The Coefficient of Variation (CoV = σ / μ) measures dispersion against 1804 empirical beam tests from Reineck database. A lower CoV indicates superior prediction accuracy.
